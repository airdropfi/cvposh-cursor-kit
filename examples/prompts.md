# Example prompts

Paste any of these into Cursor (Agent) after the `cvposh` server shows a green dot in Customize, then MCPs. All of them are read-only: nothing in your CVPosh account is changed.

1. `List the CVs in my CVPosh account.`
2. `Open my product designer CV in CVPosh and summarise my experience in three bullet points.`
3. `Check my CVPosh CV for ATS compatibility. What should I fix first?`
4. `Compare my CVPosh CV with this job posting and show the matched and missing keywords: <paste posting>`
5. `Without using my saved CVs, score this resume text for ATS: <paste text>`
6. `Suggest a tighter summary for my CVPosh CV, using only facts already in it. I will apply it in the editor.`
